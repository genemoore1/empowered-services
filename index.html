What we look for

Every HOA has different rules. We patrol against your governing documents, not a generic checklist.

Parking violations

Vehicles parked on the street, on lawns, or outside permitted areas, logged with time, location, and photo.

Front-yard equipment

Basketball goals, trailers, and other items your covenants restrict from front-yard view.

Security lighting

Some associations allow timed security lights but prohibit floodlights. We check fixtures and timing against your specific rules.

Board-ready reports

Clear, dated findings delivered to your management company or board on a set schedule.
